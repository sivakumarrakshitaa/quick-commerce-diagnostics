Quick Commerce Diagnostics

A tool I built to figure out why quick commerce orders get delayed—and what actually fixes it.

What This Is

You know how Blinkit and Zepto compete on speed? The whole business model is "10-minute delivery." But when deliveries slip to 45 minutes or fail entirely, customers leave and never come back.

So I built a tool that takes raw order data and tells you:

When do delays happen? (Evening peak? Random?)
Where do they happen? (Certain zones worse than others?)
Which product categories are slowest? (Is it always electronics?)
Why does North Delhi + Electronics + evening peak have a 42% failure rate?
What's the cheapest fix that moves the needle most?

Instead of scrolling through 10,000 order rows, you get a dashboard that shows you the exact operational levers to pull—and what happens when you pull them.
